# Icon v2 · Generation and refinement prompts

Tool: built-in imagegen. Opaque background. Initial generation followed by a localized edit to the center opening. Final image copied into the project without additional editing.

## Initial generation

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: one finished premium app icon for NAS Photo TV.
Primary request: create a new icon inspired by the visual language of Apple's Photos icon: a calm white background, a joyful rainbow rosette, translucent overlapping petals, very simple silhouette, generous breathing room. Make the design immediately read as a photo album app.
Design: one centered, exquisitely balanced six-petal rosette. Each broad softly rounded petal feels like a translucent photographic color filter or a rounded photo sheet, arranged radially with precise spacing and beautiful overlapping color transitions. Warm coral, orange, golden yellow, leaf green, ice cyan, cobalt blue and a hint of violet blend into a coherent rainbow. Use an especially fresh ice-cyan accent to connect to this app's gallery UI. The center is a small clean horizontal rounded-rectangle of white negative space, subtly suggesting a widescreen window; integrate it naturally into the flower. Six distinct outer lobes, sophisticated optical balance, clear contour readable at small size.
Style: refined minimalist app icon, mostly flat translucent color planes with very gentle luminous gradients and crisp polished edges. Cheerful, serene and contemporary. The color and translucency should do the work.
Composition: full-bleed opaque warm-white square background, one isolated flower symbol occupying approximately 65% of the square, centered with generous safe area. No baked rounded outer app mask; no surrounding device or mockup. High resolution square.
Text: none.
Avoid: a television bezel, a television stand, photo landscape mountain pictograms, extra badges, letters, Apple logo, screenshots, several variants on one page, hard black outlines, metal, deep bevels, heavy 3D, drop shadows, neon glow, dark background, decorative noise, watermark. Produce a finished usable icon, not a design presentation.
```

## Center refinement

```text
Refine this exact app icon with one localized change: reduce the central white rounded-rectangle opening to approximately 45% of its current width and 45% of its current height, remaining precisely centered and keeping its horizontal aspect ratio and soft round corners. Fill the newly freed area with a seamless natural continuation of the existing translucent petal colors, so the six petals visually converge much more closely in the center. Keep the six outer petal silhouettes, their positions, overall flower size, rainbow palette, gentle gradients, crisp edges, full-bleed opaque white background and square canvas unchanged. Add nothing else: no text, no border, no television frame or stand, no shadows. Preserve the clean Photos-inspired icon character. Return only the refined square icon.
```
